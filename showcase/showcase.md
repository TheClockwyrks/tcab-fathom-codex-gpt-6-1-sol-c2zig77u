# Fathom

You are a small light in an enormous dark. Slip through flooded corridors, grazing the plankton that brighten your body and widen your window into the abyss. Every meal helps you see—and makes you easier to find.

The maze remembers where you have been, but your view travels with you. Send a cyan sonar crest around the next corner to uncover a route or catch a glimpse of a hunter. Listen for the answering ping: the eyeless Gloamfin follows sound, including yours.

An amber light drifts through the water. It could be a harmless jellyfish worth a generous meal. It could be the Lanternjaw, patiently wearing the same disguise. Elsewhere, a Flarefish charges a sudden bloom that burns through the stone, exposing the maze and everything swimming in it.

Keep an escape route. Drop ink to break a sight hunter's pursuit, cut corners to shake the Gloamfin, and collect the final mote to clear the depth. The next dive brings more hunters and a shorter reach for your sonar. The water stays dark. Your courage carries the light.
